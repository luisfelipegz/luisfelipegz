# Luis Felipe Gutierrez

**Physics PhD | Quantitative Research | Machine Learning**

I earned my PhD in Physics from the University of Pennsylvania, where I conducted research in experimental particle physics, developing novel, model-agnostic approaches to search for new particles in complex scenarios with missing information. My research involved building an end-to-end pipeline integrating graph neural networks for regression and classification, a custom kinematic event generator, and the definition of model-agnostic signal regions using classifier outputs.

My research also involved developing and maintaining large-scale data processing and analysis pipelines using Python and C++, working with experimental data and Monte Carlo simulations. I conducted radiation studies of semiconductor devices, analyzing single-event upsets and digital and analog functionality under irradiation. My work on GaN FETs included total ionizing dose studies, heavy-ion beam experiments to measure failure rates across different linear energy transfers, and large-scale GEANT4 simulations to estimate expected device failures under HL-LHC radiation conditions.
